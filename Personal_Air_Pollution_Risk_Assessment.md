# Personal Air Pollution Risk Assessment Module
## AirSense – Air Quality Monitoring and Prediction System

---

## 1. Overview & Objective

The **Personal Air Pollution Risk Assessment** module provides individualised environmental health decision support. While standard Air Quality Index (AQI) values describe ambient urban pollution, an individual's biological risk depends on their personal exposure parameters:

$$\text{Estimated Personal Risk} = f(\text{Ambient AQI}, \text{PM Concentration}, \text{Age Demographic}, \text{Exposure Duration}, \text{Exertion Level}, \text{Pre-existing Sensitivities})$$

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Safety & Accuracy Requirement (Non-Clinical Notice):**
> The risk score generated by this system is an **Estimated Air Pollution Risk Score** for educational, lifestyle, and analytical decision support. It does **not** represent a clinical disease probability, clinical diagnosis, or medical prescription. Individual biological responses vary; users with symptoms must consult healthcare professionals.

---

## 2. Separation of Concerns (Tab Architecture)

The system maintains complete separation between environmental forecasting and personal risk assessment:

| Module / Tab | Inputs | Processing Engine | Output | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tab 1: AQI Monitoring & Prediction** | Weather, PM2.5, PM10, Gaseous Pollutants | Gradient Boosting & Random Forest Regressors | Predicted AQI & CPCB Category | Environmental Forecast |
| **Tab 2: Personal Risk Assessment** | Ambient/Predicted AQI, PM2.5, Age, Exposure Hours, Activity, Conditions | Transparent Weighted Vulnerability Model | Estimated Risk Score (0–100%), Level, Factor Breakdown, Safety Advice | Personal Protective Advisory |

---

## 3. Mathematical Scoring Model (Transparent Weights)

The risk score is normalized to a **0–100%** scale across six transparent components:

| Component | Max Points | Weight Rationale |
|---|:---:|---|
| **Ambient AQI** | 30 pts | Base urban ambient hazard level (CPCB scale). |
| **Particulate Concentration (PM2.5 / PM10)** | 20 pts | Deep lung & alveolar penetration factor. |
| **Age Sensitivity** | 15 pts | Higher physiological vulnerability in early childhood (<12y) and seniors (>60y). |
| **Exposure Duration** | 15 pts | Cumulative inhaled dose over 0.25 to 12+ hours. |
| **Physical Activity Intensity** | 10 pts | Minute ventilation volume multiplier (resting vs vigorous sports). |
| **Selected Health Conditions** | 10 pts | Pre-existing respiratory, cardiac, or chronic airway hyperreactivity. |
| **Total** | **100 pts** | Scaled to 0–100% |

---

## 4. Risk Categories and General Recommendations

| Estimated Risk Score | Category | Primary Visual Badge | General Safety Guidance | Recommended Action |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|---|---|
| **0 – 20%** | **Low** | 🟢 Green | Air quality impact on personal sensitivity is minimal. | Normal outdoor activity can generally continue. |
| **21 – 40%** | **Mild** | 🔵 Blue | Slight risk for sensitive individuals during extended exposure. | Sensitive groups should take rest breaks during long exertion. |
| **41 – 60%** | **Moderate** | 🟡 Amber | Noticeable risk for vulnerable groups during pollution peaks. | Consider reducing prolonged outdoor exposure; carry rescue inhalers if asthmatic. |
| **61 – 80%** | **High** | 🟠 Orange | Heightened exposure risk. | Consider limiting strenuous outdoor exertion; wear an N95 particulate respirator. |
| **81 – 100%** | **Very High** | 🔴 Deep Red | Severe personal exposure risk. | Avoid unnecessary outdoor exposure; stay in filtered indoor spaces. |

---

## 5. Health Conditions Supported

Users can optionally select pre-diagnosed conditions without disclosing sensitive private records:
1. **Asthma**
2. **Respiratory Sensitivity / Chronic Bronchitis**
3. **Heart-Related / Cardiovascular Conditions**
4. **Older Age (Physiological Clearance Vulnerability)**
5. **Other Pollution-Sensitive Conditions (Allergies, Post-viral recovery)**
6. **None of these (General Population)**

---

## 6. Explainability Feature

The module explicitly decomposes the score into **Aggravating Factors (+)** and **Protective Factors (-)**:
- **Increasing Factors:** Highlights high ambient AQI, elevated PM2.5 levels, prolonged exposure hours, intense athletic exertion, or asthma.
- **Reducing Factors:** Highlights low exposure duration (<30 mins), young adult demographic resilience, sedentary activity, or absence of chronic cardiopulmonary illness.
